About

Behind the lens

Dhara Visual is an independent film studio rooted in Austin, Texas.

We make films for couples, families, brands and artists, and we tell our own stories too. Weddings, proposals, music videos, corporate films, short films and feature films all start the same way for us: with people, and the moments that matter to them.

Our style is cinematic but honest. We'd rather catch the real laugh than stage a perfect one.
